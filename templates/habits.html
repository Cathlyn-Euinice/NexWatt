Fair-Play Anti-Cheat Architecture (Judge Explanation)

Judges often ask: "What if a student just clicks all the checkboxes without actually turning off their fan?"

1. Daily Points Cap (100 Pts):

A single user cannot inflate their score in one day. Consistent 7-day behavior is rewarded over one-time spamming.

2. Peer Vouching (Power Pact):

Roommates in the same hostel wing confirm each other's check-ins, adding social accountability and peer verification.

3. Streak Degradation:

Skipping days resets your streak, keeping the psychological motivation tied to genuine daily habit formation.
